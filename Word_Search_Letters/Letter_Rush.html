HOW TO PLAY
1Swipe across touching dice (any of 8 directions) to spell words of 3+ letters. Drag back to undo a letter.
2Longer words and rare letters score more. Find words quickly to build a COMBO multiplier.
3Beat the TARGET before the clock runs out. Miss it and you lose a heart. 3 hearts per run.
5Every 5th round is special: bonus tiles, long words only, lightning, or a race against a rival bot.
?Stuck? Rotate the board, use a HINT or REVEAL a word. Desktop: type a word and press Enter.
