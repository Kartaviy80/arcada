HOW TO PLAY
Tap a card to flip it, then find its twin. Matching cards fly to your collection.
Mismatches flip back — remember what you saw! Watch out for look-alikes: same picture, different color or outfit.
Bomb mismatches cost you · Clock pairs give time · Golden pairs give coins · Jokers match anything.
Special levels: Shuffle, Triple (find 3), Dark (only lantern-lit cards flip) and a Boss every 10 levels.
Power-ups: Peek shows all cards, Hint lights a pair, Freeze stops the clock.
